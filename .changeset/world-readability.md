drawn to read on your screen at any zoom, never too small or too big; a bubble
names its speaker and points to them, and where two agents talk close
together the answer sits over its speaker with the question above it, kept on
the stage and out from under its buttons. Tags of agents standing close step
out of each other's way. The chat page frames the agent you talk to near
enough to see it well, follows it round, and zooms with the wheel or a pinch
out to the whole room, above the chat on a phone. Homes are brighter at night,
the lamps reaching further. A tall thing between the camera and anyone fades
while it is in the way, as does a wall the camera goes behind. On a visit,
agents walk round you instead of through you.
