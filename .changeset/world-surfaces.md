What hangs on the walls of the 3D world is now a live page you use in the room. Task boards, screens, calendars and the lobby's kiosks are real HTML laid on their wall, sharp up close, with a body or a chair in front hiding the part behind it. Click one, or face it and press F, and the camera flies to it and holds it head-on; Escape, F, **← Step back** or a click off it flies you back. On a board you read close up, a note opens its card and dragging a note to another column (or Shift with an arrow) moves the task there at once, saved as its card saves it. The lobby's screen draws each dashboard page with the Board page's own widgets, holds still while you read it and turns with its dots or the arrows; a calendar's wakes open that agent's list. A kiosk's button presses there and then. Each has a proper screen design: a bezel and glass for screens and kiosks, a whiteboard with a marker tray, a paper calendar that takes on a home's lamplight after dark. Only surfaces big enough on the stage to read are live, eight at most; the rest, and photos, show the picture drawn of them as before. Later work can lay a surface of its own with `stage.addSurface`, described in the live-sessions doc.
