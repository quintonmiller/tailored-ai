The 3D world reads in a crowd. Round a busy meeting table, tags step aside
up or across instead of piling up, a tag that had to move has a thin line
down to its head, and in a crowd everyone's but the speaker's and the one you
point at is drawn faint. A speaker keeps its tag while it talks, and its
bubble's tail points at that tag, leaning round anyone standing in line
rather than at the body behind. A bubble pulled down under the buttons goes
beside its speaker's head, never over someone else, and in a busy room the
newest line wins over an earlier one with no room left. Pointing at any face
you can use, a model on a wall too, lights it and says "Use … · click or F",
the same thing a click does. Rearranging a room, a table brings its chairs
and a rug what stands on it (Alt moves one piece alone), and nothing you add
lands where someone stands: the `places` edit route takes `with` on a move
and `avoid` on an add. The trail of where you are keeps to one short line,
folds its middle when it must and fades while what you look at is behind it;
the map wraps names before cutting them and says which place ends a visit;
gates at a room's open side are a gateway that all but goes when someone
stands behind it; and the wheel flies you while you fly.
