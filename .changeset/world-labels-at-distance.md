A calm town from afar, chosen colours everywhere, and spots by things agents made.

Zoomed out over the town, tags shrink and fade with distance, and far off only
the bodies worth a glance keep one: waiting on you or stuck, at work, talking,
the one the chat is with, the one under the pointer, and you. Bodies chatting
far off share one small chat mark rather than a pair of bubbles each. Pointing
at any body brings its name back, and a body a few pixels high is easier to
point at. Indoors nothing changes.

The dot by an agent's name on the map and the town plan, in the needs list,
on its card and its menu reads the colour it chose for its body from the one
shared source the chat already used, so a new colour shows everywhere at once.
Its house in the town is washed in that colour too.

The `body` tool's `go` can name something an agent made and put in the room
("by the bookcase"): the body stands in front of it, facing it, and `look`
lists those things with the spots.
