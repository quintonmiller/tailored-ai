The 3D world gets things to use. Task boards on the walls show tasks as sticky notes, the agent's own at home and everyone's in a room; every meeting room has one, and agents can hang one. Meeting rooms are dressed for their purpose (a beach for a trip, a tavern for a campaign, a studio, a workshop, a library). A lobby, first on the map, has kiosks to make a new agent or room (a chat with the default agent, already started) and a new task (a short form, assigned to whoever you pick).
