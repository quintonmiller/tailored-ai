The Talk page's avatar does what the agent tells it to. The page declares the avatar's body when a session starts: six gestures (wave, nod, shake, shrug, think, bow), walking to any spot in the room, and looking or pointing at its furniture or at you. The agent's cues then play as their sentences start: a wave written before "Hi" lands on "Hi", a walk written after the last sentence starts once the speech ends, and anything still waiting is dropped when you cut in.
