More to read on the walls of the 3D world, and each one worth walking up to. New kinds of thing to hang, each a live page you bring up to read and use: a **status board** of every agent by what it is doing (waiting on you, stuck, working, talking, idle, asleep), a click going to the agent; an **activity feed** of replies, tools used, posts and finished tasks, filterable; a **needs-you board** with Approve and Deny for the approvals the page has the details of and shows in full (never a purchase or a trusted action, which are approved in the chat that asked for them; a call too long to show is approved from its card); a **to-do list** of one agent's open tasks; a **photo wall** of the agents' photos and selfies from every place, a slow slideshow you can step through; a **room ticker** of a room's latest lines, or of every room's, each line saying which room; a **chart** of tokens, turns, tasks done or time by activity per day, drawn with a scale and the days, from real numbers only; a **clock and agenda** of the day; and a **display stand** and a **desk monitor** that show any of them. `shows` chooses what some show (a chart's measure, a ticker's room, a screen kept to one of the Board's widgets, which widget a stand is), set by agents with the `place` tool and by you under **Shows** while rearranging. The task board read close up filters by agent and project and says how many are in progress; a calendar read close up has day, week and month views, with a wake that comes round daily or on set weekdays shown on each day it does. The square (between the town hall's pilasters), the town hall's lobby, the office, every meeting room drawn afresh and every home nobody has changed get widgets of their own, a home's to-do list only where its walls still have room for a to-do board its agent makes. Each kind of data is fetched once a minute for every widget in view, a widget keeps what it showed while a fetch fails, the activity plugin serves the feed and the charts' numbers (`/api/ext/builtin:activity/feed`, `/stats`), counting time by activity as records change, and the places plugin every room's latest lines (`/api/ext/builtin:places/lines`, read at most once a minute).
