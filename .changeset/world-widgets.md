More to read on the walls of the 3D world, and each one worth walking up to. New kinds of thing to hang, each a live page you bring up to read and use: a **status board** of every agent by what it is doing (waiting on you, working, talking, idle, asleep), a click going to the agent; an **activity feed** of replies, tools used, posts and finished tasks, filterable; a **needs-you board** with Approve and Deny for the approvals the page has the details of (never a purchase or a trusted action, which say where to approve them); a **to-do list** of one agent's open tasks; a **photo wall** of the agents' photos and selfies from every place, a slow slideshow you can step through; a **room ticker** of a room's latest lines; a **chart** of tokens, turns, tasks done or time by activity per day, drawn with a scale and the days, from real numbers only; a **clock and agenda** of the day; and a **display stand** and a **desk monitor** that show any of them. `shows` chooses what some show (a chart's measure, a ticker's room, a screen kept to one of the Board's widgets, which widget a stand is), set by agents with the `place` tool and by you under **Shows** while rearranging. The task board read close up filters by agent and project and says WIP; a calendar read close up has day, week and month views. The square, the town hall's lobby, the office, every meeting room drawn afresh and every new home get widgets of their own. Each kind of data is fetched once a minute for every widget in view, a widget keeps what it showed while a fetch fails, and the activity plugin serves the feed and the charts' numbers (`/api/ext/builtin:activity/feed`, `/stats`), counting time by activity as records change.
