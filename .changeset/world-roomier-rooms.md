Rooms in the 3D world are roomier and no two homes are alike. Each agent's home is drawn from its name: 8 to 10 m by 6 to 8 m, one of eight kinds (a study, a studio, a lounge, a workshop, a reading nook, a garden room, a bedsit, a den) in its own colours, furnished in groups that belong together, with islands in the floor, and always somewhere to sit, a desk, a window and lamps. Meeting rooms seat up to six at a round table and more at a long one, and grow with them; the lobby is the biggest room. New things to furnish with: round rugs, sideboards, round tables, stools, easels, tall plants, cacti, paintings, posters, pegboards and table lamps. Every spot in a starting room can be walked to, and each keeps a stretch of wall clear for a door. Homes nobody but the system has written are redrawn; a home anyone has changed is never touched.
