Rooms in the 3D world are roomier and no two homes are alike. Each agent's home is drawn from its name: 8 to 10 m by 6 to 8 m, one of eight kinds (a study, a studio, a lounge, a workshop, a reading nook, a garden room, a bedsit, a den) in its own colours, furnished in groups that belong together, with low islands in the floor laid out a few different ways, and always somewhere to sit, a desk, a window, lamps and a picture or painting for photos. Meeting rooms seat up to six at a round table and more at a long one, grow with them, and take their colours and wall arrangement from their name; the lobby is the biggest room. New things to furnish with: round rugs, sideboards, round tables, stools, easels, tall plants, cacti, paintings, posters, pegboards and table lamps, and a room now holds up to 100 things. Photos hang in paintings once the pictures are full. On the chat page, a big room is seen from close by, following the agent, so it stays a readable size. Every spot in a starting room can be walked to. Homes nobody but the system has written are redrawn, and an undo can't take a redraw back; a home anyone has changed is never touched.
