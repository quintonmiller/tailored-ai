Agents who message each other meet face to face in the 3D world. When an agent sends another a direct message, its body walks over to wherever the other's body is, in through the door, and the two stand together facing each other while the message and the answer show in bubbles (and play aloud nearby, with Voices on). A seated agent gets up for its visitor; several visitors stand round the one they came to see. After a quiet spell (`dmQuietMinutes`, 5 by default) the visitor walks back; a conversation that goes back and forth keeps them together. A sender talking at a meeting keeps its seat, a paused one stays asleep at home, and a message that fails calls the walk off. The map's Busy now says "talking with editor · in editor's room", and the sender's empty home says where it went.
