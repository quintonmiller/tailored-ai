The 3D world is a town you walk round outdoors, in place of a lobby with corridors off it. In the middle is the square: a fountain, benches and lamps, and the town hall along its north side with the kiosks in front, and the big screen, the task board and the calendar on its front. The avenue runs through the square, and off it run lanes of homes, every agent's a house of its own in its colour, with what it does standing by it (a dish on a reporter's roof, an easel by an artist's door, a cabinet of blinking lights beside a coder's) and windows lit while it is up, dark once it sleeps. The meeting rooms are in the office on the avenue, an atrium with their doors round it; a themed room has a building of its own on Main Street (a tavern, a studio, a workshop, a library) or a hut on the beach at the end of it; shared places are along Main Street; an agent's own `area` is a fenced plot at the edge of town. A stream runs down to the sea under a bridge, there is a park with a pond, and woods all round. The sky follows the hour, with the street lamps and the stars out at night. Doors into buildings go in and come out by the door you went in by; a click on a building walks you to its door; you walk faster outdoors and can zoom out over much of the town. Bodies going between places while you are out walk the streets from door to door. The map draws the town from above, every building a button, beside its list, and "where you are" reads "Town › Maple Lane › analyst's home". The places plugin lays the town out on every read (`places-town.ts`), pure and stable as agents are added, and sends its plan with the `town` place; the page builds it with a mesh or two per building, so it draws fewer calls than the lobby.
