A commitment's `nextCheck` is now read as a time or a change to watch for. With the directory on (`builtin:directory`), the `commitment` tool reads it as it's written and says what it made of it, the way `schedule` echoes a booking: "Friday 9am", "in 2 days" or "2026-10-28" become a moment in the runtime's time zone ("Check: Fri, Oct 23, 09:00, in 22h."), and "when plan/lisbon changes", "when plan changes" or "when a booking changes where status=confirmed" become a change to watch for, with a backstop a week out. Anything else stays a note: the update still goes through, and the reply says nothing will wake the agent for it and lists the forms that would. A change can only be one the agent it wakes can read. What the directory reads is kept beside the text in new commitment fields, `checkAt` (UTC), `checkOn` (`{ type, id?, where? }`), `checkedAt` and `checkEvents`, and they follow the text: a person's edit of `nextCheck` on the State page, the agent's page or a room's page is read again the same way. On a room's commitment, a person's `nextCheck` holds like their other edits; the fields beside it never do. Commitments written before this keep their text with nothing beside it until it's next written.

The agent page's Working on list shows how far off each check is (or "(a note)" for text nothing wakes the agent for) and when it was last checked, and pressing the check changes it. A room's page shows the same, and its Edit form takes the next check; `PATCH rooms/:room/commitments/:id` accepts `nextCheck`.

`schedule`'s `when` (and so a check's time) also takes a weekday: "friday", "friday 9am", "mon at 14:00", "next friday", meaning the next one whose time is still ahead, 09:00 when no time is given.

Commitments now wake their owner when their check comes due. Every thirty seconds the directory looks for open and waiting commitments whose `checkAt` has come and wakes each one's owner (a room's lead, for a room's own), one at a time, through `runtime.wakeAgent`, in the session `commitment:<id>` with `wake.kind: "commitment"`, so the pause, an online agent's own switch and its hourly wake ceiling apply, and each check counts as one of its `rooms.maxWakesPerHour`. A check is claimed before its turn (its time taken off, `checkedAt` set), so a slow turn never runs twice, across a reload too. Paused, it stays due; at the ceiling it comes round five minutes on. A change a commitment watches for makes it due at once with the change's line kept, and a burst of changes becomes one wake that lists them; a change counts only when the agent can read what changed and it wasn't its own, and a week with none wakes it anyway. The turn is told the commitment, how to tell it's done, its next step, its three newest updates, why it's due and what it may do, in about 120 tokens, and asked to do the next step and update the commitment with a new check. A turn that writes no update gets "Checked; no change." written for it, which doesn't count as the work moving ("last update", the page and upkeep's "no update in a while" skip it); with no new check, a check on a time comes round 1, 2, 4, then every 7 days, and a check on a change goes back to waiting with its week's backstop. Finished, dropped and archived commitments never wake, nor does an agent with no `commitment` tool. Upkeep no longer reports a check the directory read as come, since the check itself wakes the agent.

Behaviour you will notice: until autonomy is enforced, every check's turn asks first, whatever the commitment's `autonomy` says, so `tell` and `quiet` behave like `suggest`. A call in such a turn that writes, can't be undone, or declares no effect is refused through `agent.pre_tool_use` with "This commitment asks first: put it in nextStep and set the commitment waiting on a person.", except the agent's own `commitment`, `writeup` and `directory` and calls that declare they only read. Most tools declare no effect yet, so most are refused there. A person's chat and every other turn are unaffected.
