Agents are in charge of their own bodies in the 3D world. A new `body` tool, which an agent with a `tools:` list names like any other, lets an agent look itself over and choose its colour (a name or a hex, in place of the one its name gives it), the model it is drawn in from those the deployment has, its size, and what it wears; how it idles (calm, lively, focused, sleepy), how it stands (upright, slouched, proud, shy, arms folded), how it walks (brisk, stroll, bouncy, sneak, march, swagger) and what it likes doing when idle. It can make a gesture now, once or for a while: wave, nod, shake, cheer, clap, dance, bow, shrug, think, point, salute, stretch, jump or laugh, turned to you or to whoever it means. And it can go to a spot in words ("sit on the couch", "by the window") and stay there until it chooses otherwise or a time it gives runs out, or to another place for a while. Its work and its conversations still come first: it goes to its desk to work, or to whoever it is talking to, and walks back after. Staying at a spot, it does what the spot offers, one thing after another; a body that chose the same spot as another, or finds someone working or talking there, waits until it is free.

Everything it chooses is one `body` record per agent, so a restart keeps it, and the page follows it live: a new colour, model or size remakes the body where it stands without rebuilding the room, and the rest changes on the body as it is. Idle styles, postures and walks feed the same animation as before: the files play a crouched walk for a sneak and a formal one for a march when they have them, a library's own dance clip for a dance, and the code's own moves otherwise, which now take the arms over properly, turned on a model made in an A-pose as they would be on one made in a T-pose, so a wave is up and a clap's hands meet. On an agent's card, a Body row opens the same choices. The dot by an agent's name, in the chat, the needs list, a place's card and on boards and calendars, takes the colour it chose. An agent that never uses the tool looks and moves as it always did.

What a body wears moves into the body record. The `place` tool's `room` action still takes `wear`, and writes it there; an old `wear` record is read until the agent or a person chooses again, and taken away then. `GET /api/live/models` lists the models a body can be drawn in, every one the config names and every `.vrm` file in `<TAI_HOME>/avatars/`, and `GET /api/live/avatar?model=` serves one by name. Voice cues offer the new gestures too.
