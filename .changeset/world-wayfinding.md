Find your way round the 3D world. Walking, the wheel or a pinch zooms the camera following you from close behind out to a whole hall or the lobby; it starts wider than before, frames you in the part of the stage the chat panel leaves, and keeps your zoom while the page is open. The lobby, the halls and the meeting rooms stay brightly lit after dark while homes keep their cosy night. A door under the pointer lights up and says where it goes, and a long name on a door's plate shrinks or wraps onto two lines instead of being squeezed. A trail under the stage's buttons says where you are ("Main building › Residences E–N › analyst's home"), each step a click away. The map leads with people and rooms: who is busy, every agent coloured by what it is doing, meeting rooms, shared places and other areas, with the halls folded away; typing filters it and Enter goes to the first match. The places plugin's map lists what each place is reached through (`via`) for the trail.
