Idle agents look alive, and work shows up where it happens. An idle body passes the time with what its room has, weighted by the hour and drawn per agent: it reads by the bookshelf, waters a plant, looks out of a window, studies the task board, makes a coffee at the sideboard, stretches, tidies the desk, and naps on the couch late at night. Point at a body, or walk up to it, and its tag says what it is up to, what it did last ("last: searched the web, 40 min ago") and when it next wakes ("next: 07:00"); a sleeping one's says why and until when. The activity plugin keeps `last`, `next` (the soonest wake booked with `schedule`, or an online agent's next turn) and `until` on idle and sleeping agents' records. A working agent goes to its desk, or the task board for tasks (the desk in a room without one). When an agent talks in a room, its body takes a seat in that room's meeting room, with its speech bubbles, and goes back where it lives after a quiet spell (`quietMinutes` in the places plugin's config, 10 by default, 0 to turn off). A visit is a memory-backed `visit` record, so it never changes where the agent lives and leaves no history. The map lists who is busy at the top, most pressing first, with where each is, and marks the places where something is happening.
