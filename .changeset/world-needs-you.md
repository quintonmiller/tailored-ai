Answer what agents are waiting on you for without leaving the 3D world. While any agent waits, a **Needs you · N** button joins the stage's tools: it lists every request (approvals first, then the longest wait) with who, what and how long, and a row takes you into the agent's place, standing in front of it, with a card for the request open. Clicking a body with a `!` opens the same card, and the map's Busy now goes there too. The card shows a tool call as the chat's approval panel does, every argument and the raw call, with Approve or Deny and an optional note; anything that may spend money, or has an approval of its own, is never approvable there: it shows its details, Deny, and that it is approved in the chat that asked for it. Approve is offered only for a request whose details the server sent, and never on the second click of a double click. A question on a task is answered as the Tasks page answers it, back to the backlog with your answer as a comment; any other question opens the conversation it asked in, with the question quoted. What you did stays on the card, the `!` goes as the agent's activity changes, and a request answered somewhere else leaves the world without a reload. The activity plugin's record now says what an agent waits on (`request`, `task`, the whole `question`, and the `session` it was asked in), stops waiting once that task moves out of blocked, and stops only for the approval it was waiting on.
